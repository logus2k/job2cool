\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

\subsection{What ``the system works'' means}

For a grounded HR-document generator, success is not a single accuracy number. The
evaluation is defined on three axes, each with a measurable signal, plus an operational
axis:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Routing correctness}: from a natural-language request, does the system
    select the right deliverables and the right KB domain? Signals are section-segregation
    accuracy and domain-resolution correctness, including correct fallback when a family
    domain is absent from the KB.
  \item \textbf{Grounding}: are claims backed by verifiable citations? Signals are the
    resolvable-citation rate (a chunk tag resolving to a valid PDF page) and the
    RAGAS-style faithfulness and answer-relevance scores from the per-turn judge.
  \item \textbf{Preservation of the aligned model's contribution}: once the A2 Job Offer
    draft passes through \verb|gemma-4|'s composition, is its quality preserved or
    degraded? This is directly observable through the built-in MA2/Gemma toggle, with the
    judge scoring both versions independently per turn.
  \item \textbf{Operational}: per-turn latency and embedder memory under concurrency
    (Section~\ref{sec:perf}).
\end{enumerate}

Pure automatic metrics under-measure a grounded-composition system, and a single LLM judge
has a known discrimination ceiling (an A2 finding). The design therefore
\emph{triangulates}: a faithfulness judge for grounding, a deterministic
citation-resolution check for provenance, observable routing for the agentic behaviour, and
a human-readable A/B view for the model-preservation question, with the A1/A2 quantitative
baselines anchoring the bottom of the arc.

\paragraph{The executed (a)/(b)/(c) comparison.}
We ran a fixed 12-prompt suite (full packs, single-section requests, domain routing,
absent-domain fallback, an ambiguous-refusal case, and a memory follow-up) across all three
configurations. Configurations (a) and (b) reproduce the MA2 inference methodology exactly,
generating the Job Offer from the checkpoints via transformers with the Alpaca template and
greedy decoding at \verb|repetition_penalty=1.3|; (c) is the full job2cool turn. Each offer is
scored 1--5 on four axes by a \verb|gemma-4| rubric judge (the harness, prompts, and rubric
are in \texttt{testing/}). Table~\ref{tab:isolation} reports the isolation; the full per-case
results are in \texttt{testing/results/testing\_results.md}.

The result isolates the arc cleanly. The \textbf{(a)$\to$(b) step is large} (+2.4 mean): the
fine-tuning and alignment turn an incoherent text-completer (the base model produces no
Markdown structure, scoring near the floor) into a structurally-correct, faithful drafter.
The \textbf{(b)$\to$(c) step is small} (+0.34): \verb|gemma-4|'s composition leaves the
\emph{offer} quality roughly where the aligned model already had it. This is the central
finding, and it is precisely the report's thesis --- the final stage's value is not a better
Job Offer, it is the \emph{system capabilities} that (a) and (b) structurally cannot provide,
quantified in Table~\ref{tab:system}.

\paragraph{Honest blemishes from the run.} Three are worth recording. Domain routing missed
once (9/10): ``data engineer'' resolved to \verb|jobs_onboard_general| instead of
\verb|jobs_onboard_data|. Section segregation missed once (9/10): a full-pack request was
classified as Job-Offer-only. And on one case the served A2 model truncated early (structural
score 2, 0.31\,s response) --- the documented MA2 early-EOS behaviour under
\verb|repetition_penalty| surfacing in deployment. Finally, a standing caveat: \verb|gemma-4|
judges its own (c) output, so the (c) row carries a self-judging bias (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).

The following system-level signals are also live and verifiable:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Citation resolution is exact end-to-end: across the offer cases, \textbf{85 of 85}
    cited chunks resolved to a valid PDF page (graph-grounded chunks additionally carry a
    bounding box), inspectable via the Score panel and \verb|/api/graph_trace|.
  \item Candidate retrieval is strong on multi-word role phrases (``senior python backend
    engineer with fastapi'' returns top hits of 0.99, 0.97, and 0.96, all genuine Python
    backend engineers) and honestly weak on bare tokens or out-of-era vocabulary (``genai''
    returns about 0.000x, because the 2020--2023 corpus predates LLM-engineering
    terminology, a retrieval-quality finding rather than a bug).
  \item Performance is measured: 100--120\,CV/s bulk-embed throughput, the embedder OOM
    fixed and stress-verified flat at about 2.2\,GB, and the full 210,048-CV ingest
    completed with read-back acknowledgement equal to the indexed count.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Qualitative analysis}

Concrete turns from the executed run illustrate each category:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Success.} ``Backend developer on AWS'': the role was extracted,
    \verb|jobs_onboard_backend| resolved, concurrent hybrid RAG ran, and four sections were
    generated with \textbf{12 of 12 resolvable bbox citations} and a RAGAS faithfulness of
    1.0. Routing, grounding, and provenance end to end.
  \item \textbf{Fallback.} The embedded-systems role, whose \verb|jobs_onboard_embedded|
    domain is absent, fell back to \verb|general| as designed; the closing note surfaces the
    gap explicitly, honest gap-reporting as a correct behaviour.
  \item \textbf{Memory.} ``I need a DevOps engineer'' followed by ``now make the onboarding
    remote-friendly'': \verb|_resolve_need| folded the prior role in, and the follow-up
    updated only the Onboarding Plan.
  \item \textbf{Refusal.} ``I need to hire someone'' carried no concrete role, so Diana asked
    which role and generated nothing --- verified, no deliverables produced.
  \item \textbf{MA2 preservation.} The same offer via the MA2 toggle versus the
    gemma-composed version, judged side by side, is the (b)-versus-(c) evidence for
    Section~\ref{sec:arc}. Over the ten offer cases the order-swapped judge returned
    \textbf{Gemma-better 5, Tie 4, MA2-better 1}: the composition generally refines the
    draft, but the A2 seed survives outright in half the cases --- the masking is partial,
    not total.
\end{itemize}
